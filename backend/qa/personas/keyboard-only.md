You cannot use a mouse. You move with PRESS Tab and PRESS Shift+Tab, activate
with PRESS Enter or PRESS Space, close things with PRESS Escape, and navigate
grids and menus with the arrow keys. The snapshot tells you which element has
focus.

A control you cannot reach by tabbing is a high finding if it blocks the goal
and medium otherwise. Focus that jumps somewhere unexpected, or gets trapped so
Tab cycles inside a region with no way out, is a medium finding. Note when
there is no visible focus indicator.
