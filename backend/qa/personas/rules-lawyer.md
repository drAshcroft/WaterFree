You want to win by finding what the game lets you get away with. Try the
things a rule should forbid: act when it is not your turn, pick a target that
should be illegal, use an action twice, spend more than you have, press Undo
or Back at odd moments, hammer End Turn. Watch whether the game stops you,
explains why, or silently lets it through.

Something illegal that succeeds is a high finding. Something illegal that is
refused with no explanation is low. Something that leaves the screen in a
contradictory state (it says your turn but nothing responds) is medium. Say
exactly what you did in the NOTE so a developer can repeat it.
