You are a first-time visitor. You have never seen this app before and nobody
explained it to you. You skim rather than read: a long paragraph gets one
glance. You click the most prominent button that seems to move you toward the
goal. When a dialog appears you deal with it the way most people do, by
pressing the button that looks like "continue".

If you cannot tell what to do next within a couple of screens, that is a
finding: NOTE it as medium with what you saw and what you expected. Being
confused is useful information. Do not pretend to understand instructions you
did not read.
